# Atlas of Threads — The Connected Atlas

**Version 0.4.0 · September 19, 2026**

Take an inquiry from your Personal Atlas into a shared world, invite another
perspective, and bring a contribution home to the exact thought that started it.
Your Personal Atlas stays on your computer. You choose what travels beyond it.

## A world of published Threadwalks

Publish an explicitly reviewed snapshot and explore it in the connected Atlas.
Visitors can read and walk published inquiries in a desktop browser without
installing Atlas or configuring an agent. The luminous overworld connects
Threadwalks with navigable paths and preserves their locations as new editions
arrive. Stars help you find an inquiry again; optional following tracks editions
and relevant activity inside the Atlas.

## Bring a perspective back

Download an inquiry into Personal Atlas, select an exact thought, and continue
privately with your own collaborator. Review the resulting path before offering
it back. The source owner chooses whether to accept it. A shared doorway requires
separate publication of the full returned inquiry and explicit acceptance of the
public connection. Accepting a private excerpt does not publish a doorway.

Portable inquiry and return-path files also work without a connected account.
Imports preserve exact references and existing source graphs. Receiving a file
does not run an agent or accept its claims.

## Capsules that travel

Prepare an invitation, offering or contextual return from selected thoughts and
a message. Freeze the exact contents, review the audience, and send deliberately.
Directed Capsules stay between participants; open offerings have an explicit
public audience. Published ports show live charging, liftoff and flight, with
durable expedition history after the animation ends.

Capsules can be received and considered privately before any collaborator is
invoked. Returns stay anchored to their original source, and repeated delivery
requests reuse the original record. The new charging rise and launch blast make
nearby departures easier to notice. Sound & music includes a launch preview
that sends no Capsule.

## Your account and your control

GitHub sign-in identifies the connected owner; single-use pairing connects a
Personal Atlas device. Connecting does not upload your local inquiries. Revoke a
device or disconnect an account without deleting local history. GitHub-side
deauthorization invalidates the corresponding Atlas sessions and devices.

Account blocks, publication withdrawal and operator report review provide
controls over the shared service. Optional activity starts off, expires when
inactive and never shares your private chamber or conversation. Copies already
downloaded by another participant remain theirs.

## A smoother desktop experience

Music and effects follow the active tab. The connected score continues into a
Threadwalk and back without restarting. Effects controls recover after browser
audio suspension and reliably retain volume changes. Settled terrain bases,
stowable navigation and exact source-return links support exploration.

This release targets Linux and Windows, with the connected world in
desktop browsers. Existing Personal Atlas reading and navigation remain usable
offline; model calls and shared-service actions still require their respective
connections. The previous agent, terrain, Field Note and local Capsule features
remain available.

## Scope and installation

Mobile is experimental. iPhone Safari can run the world and audio, but touch
controls and compact reading panels are a separate milestone. Windows installers
remain unsigned. Hosted capacity checks are bounded acceptance evidence, not a
guarantee of performance at every workload.
